05 · Momentum matters

Protect the momentum toward purchase

Discover

Compare

Evaluate

Confidence

Purchase

By the time they click “Proceed to Checkout”, users have already:

✓ Found a product

✓ Compared alternatives

✓ Evaluated reviews

✓ Accepted the price

✓ Added it to the cart

Unrelated promotions now only invite them to reconsider.

Shopping is a continuous flow, not isolated actions. Each successful interaction builds momentum toward the next. Presenting unrelated promotions or encouraging more browsing at checkout resets part of the decision process. The most effective interfaces remove distractions as purchase intent increases.

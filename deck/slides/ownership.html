Where each team’s content belongs

TeamWants visibleWhere it earns its place
MarketingCampaigns, promo bannersAwareness and loyalty; never checkout
MerchandisingFeatured products, collectionsExploration; a few on the homepage
Product contentSpecs, reviews, FAQsEvaluation and decision
LoyaltyRewards, membershipsPost-purchase and returning users
SupportChat, help centreOn demand; prominent after purchase

The ground rule

Visibility is earned by relevance to the user’s current stage, not by team priority.

The paper describes how digital products accumulate features as each team competes for visibility, ending in a homepage where every component claims equal importance. This slide gives the team a shared answer to “where should my thing go?”. It is a suggested mapping based on the journey stages in the paper; agree the final version with stakeholders.

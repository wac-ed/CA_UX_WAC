Show = prominent · Low = available, not prominent · Hide = out of view · tune per business model

This expands the contextual-feature-exposure principle into a grid the team can use in design reviews. It is derived from the paper’s guidance: wishlists matter in exploration and evaluation; comparison before the decision, not at payment; coupons in cart and checkout; order tracking after purchase; recommendations early, never at checkout, and only after confirmation post-purchase; delivery and return information builds trust at every stage; navigation and promotions are minimised in checkout. Treat individual cells as a starting point to debate, not a rule. A luxury brand and a marketplace would fill some cells differently.

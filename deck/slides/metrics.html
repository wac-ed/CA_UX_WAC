One metric to watch per stage

StageWatchA healthy signal
AwarenessHomepage bounce rateFewer quick exits
ExplorationZero-result and exit searchesSearches lead to products
EvaluationProduct page → add to cartMore confident adds
DecisionCart → checkout startFewer stalled carts
PurchaseCheckout completion, form errorsHigher completion
Post-purchase“Where is my order?” contactsFewer anxious contacts
LoyaltyRepeat purchase rateMore returning buyers
The paper argues UX is a measurable driver of business performance but does not prescribe metrics. These are suggested KPIs that map to each stage’s goal, chosen so the team can tell whether CAUX changes are working. Baseline them before redesigning, then compare after each change. Post-purchase support contacts tie to the paper’s point that a reassuring confirmation experience reduces anxiety and support requests.

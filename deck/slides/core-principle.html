06 · The Context-Aware UX Framework · Core principle

Every interface element should justify its presence by helping the user achieve their immediate goal.

CAUX doesn’t remove features. It presents them when they are most relevant.

Most interfaces are static: the same navigation, banners, recommendation widgets, wishlists and campaigns persist on every page. CAUX designs around user intent instead of pages, features or business departments. Example: a wishlist is valuable, but a customer entering payment probably doesn’t need it. A weekend-sale banner may work on the homepage, but at checkout it introduces an alternative decision at the exact moment the user has committed.

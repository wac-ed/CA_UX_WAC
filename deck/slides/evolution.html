03 · The evolution of e-commerce UX

From catalogs to clarity

STAGE 1

Transaction-centric

Digital catalogs. If users could find it and order it, the site worked.

STAGE 2

Experience-centric

Smarter search, better recommendations, simpler checkout. UX becomes a business driver.

STAGE 3

Feature overload

Flash sales, loyalty, AI assistants, wishlists, social proof, all at once.

THE NEXT STEP

Clarity over complexity

Focused interactions that match each user’s current goal.

UX now moves metrics:

navigation → discovery · product pages → confidence · checkout → lower abandonment

Early online retail won on availability, price and the ability to transact. As competition intensified and switching became easy, experience became the differentiator: the shift from transaction-centric to experience-centric commerce. Today UX influences nearly every critical metric, and poor UX can cancel out investments in marketing, pricing or product quality. But in chasing engagement, platforms keep adding features. Each is valuable alone; shown together they cause overload. More functionality does not automatically create a better experience. The best platforms prioritise clarity over complexity.

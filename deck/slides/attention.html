05 · The psychology of online shopping

Every element competes for limited attention

Hero carousel

Flash sale banner

Discount popup

Newsletter signup

Loyalty rewards

Recommended

Trending

Recently viewed

Floating chat widget

Multiple menus

10 elements.
1 user’s attention.

Each has a valid business purpose. The issue is that they are all visible at once.

More load = slower, less confident users who abandon.

Human working memory has limited capacity, and every banner, button, notification or card asks users to process information and decide. This imagined homepage holds a hero carousel, flash sale banner, discount popup, newsletter signup, loyalty rewards, recommended, trending and recently viewed products, a floating chat widget and multiple navigation menus. Good UX is not about adding functionality; it is about reducing unnecessary mental effort.

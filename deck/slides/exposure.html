Not every feature should stay visible throughout the journey; visibility should follow intent. Exposing features contextually keeps interfaces focused without sacrificing functionality. Progressive disclosure means users don’t need everything at once: an initial product card needs only image, name, price and rating. Detailed specifications, warranty information and reviews become relevant once the user signals interest by opening the product page.

UX priorities differ because business objectives differ. Patterns don’t transfer automatically.

Not all e-commerce platforms serve the same purpose, and borrowing patterns from a successful platform without its context is a common mistake. A Samsung visitor compares Galaxy phones, learns about new tech and sees how products integrate: the experience is educational and reinforces trust. An Amazon visitor is rarely brand-loyal and wants the best product fast, comparing price, reviews, delivery and seller credibility: efficiency beats storytelling.
